How It Works

Five steps. One clear process.

The workflow is intentionally simple so a new partner can understand what happens next.

01 / REGISTER
📝

Register

Complete your partner registration and share your basic details.

02 / TRAINING
🎓

Understand opportunities, customer conversation and work process.

03 / ASSIGNMENT
📋

View available campaigns, work or customer enquiries as applicable.

📞

Work & Follow Up

Contact customers and complete the assigned work professionally.

05 / TRACK
📊

Submit & Track

Submit work status and track eligible performance or commission information.
