FAQ

Questions before you start?

What is KARTIK NETWORK?

KARTIK NETWORK is a structured partner network covering eligible opportunities across Real Estate, Finance and Digital Services.

What are the main opportunity categories?

The three core categories are Real Estate, Finance and Digital Services.

What happens after registration?

The partner journey can include training, opportunity selection, assigned work, customer follow-up, work submission and tracking.

Is work or opportunity guaranteed after registration?

No. Opportunity availability, eligibility and work assignment can depend on the applicable business arrangement.

How can I contact KARTIK NETWORK?

You can submit the partner registration form or contact the KARTIK NETWORK team through WhatsApp.
