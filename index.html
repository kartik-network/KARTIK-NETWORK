FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Kartik Network?

Kartik Network is being developed as a business and partner network connecting opportunities across Real Estate, Finance and Digital Marketing.

How can I become a partner?

Contact our team through WhatsApp or phone. The future platform will provide digital partner registration and onboarding.

Can I work in more than one category?

The planned system will allow eligible partners to explore multiple available campaigns based on requirements, training and approval.

How will commission work?

Commission will depend on the individual campaign and applicable business terms. The future platform will record eligible commission milestones and payout status.

Is there a fixed salary?

Partner opportunities may be campaign-based and can differ from employee roles. Specific terms will always be communicated before participation in a campaign.
